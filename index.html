About

One man, a bench,
and the timber.

James Hagley has been working with wood for twenty years. He started as a carpenter’s apprentice in Devon, learning the joinery that underpins every piece he makes today — hand-cut dovetails, through-tenons, drawbore pegging. The first decade taught him how buildings move; the last five, working for himself, have taught him how furniture should too.

There is no workshop team, no showroom, no catalogue. Just James at his bench in Honiton, listening to what you need and designing a piece around it. He sources timber from local sawmills wherever possible — English oak, brown oak, ash, cherry, walnut — and finishes every surface by hand. The work is slow, honest, and built to last longer than its owner.
